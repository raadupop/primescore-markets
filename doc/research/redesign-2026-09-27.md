# Redesign after the refuted signal: 27 to 28 September 2026

What was wrong, what was tested, what was built. Decision record: `doc/adr/0008-directional-scenario-refuted.md`.
Tests and results: `doc/research/preregistration-2026-09-27.md`. First outcome test: `doc/research/edge-test-2026-09-27.md`.

## 1. The design problem

One variable under five names, and no place where a decision met an outcome.

| Name in the product | What it actually is | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Severity | Rank of today's level among the previous 1,260 closes of the same index, signed by above or below the median | `apps/classification/app/strategies/market_data.py` |
| Composite | With only market data classified, the strongest confirmed severity | `Modules/Classification/Aggregation/CompositeCalculator.cs` |
| Scenario | Observed level × (1 + composite × k) | `Modules/Classification/Aggregation/DislocationCalculator.cs` |
| Dislocation | Observed level × composite × k: the level times a rank of the level | same |
| Conditions met | Exactly an absolute composite of at least 0.5 on all 15,849 recorded decisions; every other condition was implied or always true | `Modules/Decision/Evaluation/DecisionEvaluator.cs` (before ADR-0008) |

- **Direction was declared, never estimated.** The SRS sign convention reads above-median as "vol expansion". Volatility indices mean-revert from stretched levels, so the signalled direction was right 34 to 41 percent of the time.
- **A rank fires on a fixed share of days.** An absolute score of at least 0.5 holds on the upper half of any window by construction: 55 percent of VIX days. The SRS's own DEC-002 check (idle on at least 70 percent of days) failed.
- **The event thesis was never in the data.** Macro prints waited for an empty consensus file; cross-asset and geopolitical routes return 501. Every composite had one category.
- **The validation could not fail.** Every event was dated on the close after a spike, where the level rank is at or near the top of its window (0.9992 on 5 February 2018).

The mathematics is correct and the code reproduces it exactly; the specification gave a descriptive statistic a predictive meaning.

## 2. What the tests established

Four hypotheses, each designed by one agent, frozen in a pre-registration, fitted on 2011 to 2018, run once on 2019 onward, and attacked by two adversaries. Directional claims were measured on VX futures, which are tradable, not on the spot index.

| Hypothesis | Holdout result | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VX futures carry: steep curve predicts the 5-day front-month move | 48 trades, −0.56 points per trade net, 42 percent hit, below the placebo median | KILL |
| Catalyst crush: VX30 falls from the settle before FOMC or CPI to the release-day settle | 149 events, excess −0.10 points, p 0.095, tradable +0.03 net of cost | INCONCLUSIVE (closest miss) |
| Variance premium: a stretched state compresses implied minus realized | 45 events, Delta +0.018 in the wrong direction, p 0.60 | KILL |
| Magnitude: the state predicts larger tradable moves beyond recent realized | No grid cell passed on 2011 to 2018 | KILL in sample |

No hypothesis supports a predictive rule from free data. The spot reversion after extreme states is real, but VX futures already price it.

## 3. What was built

The engine is now an honest state engine with a measured record.

- **State gate.** A decision fires when the level is within 5 percent of either end of its five-year history (`level_percentile_tail`), with at least 252 prior closes. It records `extreme_high`, `high`, `normal`, `low` or `extreme_low`, with no direction. The dislocation is recorded for reproducibility and no longer gates.
- **Outcomes record.** `GET /analytics/outcomes`, the Outcomes page and `outcomes-report` measure the reference change 1, 5, 10 and 21 trading days after every decision, from the live journal or a stored replay. The same figures come from the ledger each time.
- **Automatic upgrade.** A stored configuration with the old gate gains the state gate as a new, audited version on start, keeping every other threshold. Old decisions keep their labels and are always shown as legacy.
- **Contract 1.2.0.** Adds the outcomes endpoint, the decision's `state` and `level_percentile`, and the dislocation's `regime` and `regime_percentile`.
- **Dashboard and product page.** "Conditions met" became "All checks passed" beside a separate state label, legacy records are marked as legacy, the scenario panels became percentile and state, and the public slider that multiplied the level by a rank of itself was removed.

Proof on a copy of the live ledger, replaying 2016 to 2026 under the new gate:

| Market | Extreme-state days | Share of days |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VIX | 363 of 2,695 | 13.5 percent |
| OVX | 264 of 2,695 | 9.8 percent |

| VIX state | Days | Median change after 21 days | Days higher after 21 days |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Extremely stretched | 126 | −9.27 | 13 percent |
| Stretched | 739 | −2.51 | 33 percent |
| Normal | 978 | −0.30 | 45 percent |
| Compressed | 615 | +0.61 | 61 percent |
| Extremely compressed | 237 | +0.63 | 72 percent |

After 21 trading days the level had moved back toward its median on 77 percent of VIX extreme days
(95 percent block interval 67 to 87) and 76 percent of OVX extreme days (64 to 89). These figures are
descriptive and in sample; the 5 percent tail was chosen for alert frequency, not fitted; the futures
carry test shows the reversion is priced. Days are NYSE trading days with a close, so holiday-session
prints are left out.
