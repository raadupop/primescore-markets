# Edge test T1, preliminary: 27 September 2026

Test from `doc/briefs/markets-product-study.md` §4 T1, run on the 15,849 decisions already recorded
in `apps/engine/var/engine.db` (configuration version 2, uncalibrated defaults). Reproduce with
`python scripts/edge_test.py`. One state per market per New York date: the last decision recorded
that date. Forward change is the reference index close h trading days later minus that date's
close. Hit means the change has the sign of the combined score. Intervals are 95 percent
bootstraps over 21-day blocks.

## What it means

1. **T1 fails as designed.** A positive score reads "volatility expansion", but the index usually
   falls afterwards. The classifier detects stretched states; volatility indices mean-revert from
   them. The scenario multiplier extrapolates in the wrong direction.
2. **The conditions are not an alert.** **Conditions met** on more than half of VIX days cannot
   be sold as a timely signal.
3. **The inverse is information, not yet edge.** "Stretched volatility mean-reverts" is well known
   and largely priced: the VIX index is not tradable, and VIX futures and options already price the
   expected reversion through the term structure. Spot-index hit rates overstate what a tradable
   instrument captures.
4. **OVX magnitude.** On OVX **Conditions met** days, the median absolute 5-day move is 2.84 points
   against 2.14 on all days. Signal days precede larger moves in either direction; that is the only
   reading relevant to long-volatility structures, and it is untested against option prices.
