01 / How it works

Every result.
A path back to evidence.

Four stages connect the market observation to your review. A research signal identifies conditions to investigate; it does not open a position.

The research workflowData → Signals → State → Outcomes
  1. 01

    Data

    Start with an observed index level, its source and the time it was recorded.

    ProducesA traceable observation
  2. 02

    Signals

    Compare the observation with prior history to describe its position against its median and how unusual it is.

    ProducesA score with reasoning
  3. 03

    State

    Place the level in its five-year history: compressed, normal, stretched, or extreme at either end.

    ProducesA state, not a forecast
  4. 04

    Outcomes

    Measure what the index did after every recorded state, published whatever it shows.

    ProducesAn auditable record
/ A

Consistent classification.

Market and macroeconomic observations scored against their reference history.

/ B

Traceable reasoning.

Source provenance, certainty and the calculation behind each assessment.

/ C

Repeatable replay.

Return to the same observation, inspect its context, and reproduce the result.

02 / Research boundaries

Know what
the result can tell you.

The workspace records data, states and their measured outcomes. No tested rule has shown a tradable edge yet; every test and its result is written down, failures included.

  1. 01

    US equities and crude oil

    VIX and OVX are reference volatility indices. The workspace does not select an option contract, expiry, strike or position size.

    Current scope
  2. 02

    Broader evidence

    Market history is available. Missing macro consensus remains missing; cross-asset and geopolitical classification require further work.

    Incomplete
  3. 03

    Historical evaluation

    The outcomes record measures index changes after each state. It does not measure portfolio returns. Stretched levels tend to revert in the record, but no tested rule turned that into a tradable edge on VIX futures; OVX has no futures in the test.

    Research only
