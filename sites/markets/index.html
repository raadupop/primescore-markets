Event-driven
volatility intelligence.

Market, macroeconomic and geopolitical events can change expectations of future volatility. PrimeScore Markets is being developed to classify those events and investigate volatility dislocations for options research and trading.

Today: market and macro classification, a local historical replay demo and executable engineering controls. The full engine and geopolitical AI are planned.

From an event
to a research question.

An event's importance and an option market's pricing are different questions. The platform is designed to connect them while retaining the source, scoring assumptions and uncertainty. Predictive usefulness still requires independent outcome validation.

Planned engine: live market / macro / geopolitical feeds → .NET ingestion → Python classification → corroborated composite → independently evaluated volatility forecast / dislocation → decisions, positions and risk controls.

Engineering approach

Built with agents.
Checked against constraints.

The repository explores agentic AI systems operating under upfront constraints and runtime validation. Architecture contracts constrain dependencies. Tool hooks check code and contracts. A bounded steering loop reports failures and escalates stalled retries; ADRs preserve decisions.

These controls operate during development. The implemented classifier uses statistical methods; geopolitical language-model inference and RAG remain planned. Passing checks establishes the properties those checks cover, not the economic value of a signal.
