AI that scores things.
So people make
better decisions.

PrimeScore AI turns information into scores that people can inspect and question. The aim is to make the evidence, assumptions and uncertainty behind a decision visible. Its first product explores event-driven volatility intelligence for options research.

A score should
be inspectable.

A number alone is not enough. The work pairs scores with source information, uncertainty and a trace of how the result was produced.

01 / EVIDENCEKeep the source visible

Connect an observation to its origin and the point in time it represents.

02 / SCORINGExpose the assumptions

Show the method, the available history and the limits of the result.

03 / JUDGEMENTSupport the decision

Give people information to examine, with uncertainty they can take into account.
